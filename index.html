Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the monthly rent?

Room, meals, Wi-Fi, housekeeping, RO water and power backup. Please call us to confirm the current inclusions for your room type.

Is there a security deposit?

A refundable security deposit is usually collected at joining. Contact us for the exact amount.

Are visitors allowed?

Yes, visitors are allowed in the common area during visiting hours and are noted in the visitor register.

Can I visit before booking?

Of course! Call or WhatsApp us to schedule a visit and see the rooms yourself.
